BrainVolt - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use BrainVolt, a mobile game that includes a Question Game and an Arrow Puzzle.

1. About BrainVolt

BrainVolt provides offline, single-player quiz and puzzle gameplay. The current release does not require users to create an account and does not provide online multiplayer or cloud synchronisation.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress and settings, such as levels, scores, lives, hints, achievements and preferences, may be stored locally on your device so that the game can remember your progress. This information is not intended to be sent to BrainVolt servers in the current release.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored game information.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

BrainVolt uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. These may include banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. If you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, the app may provide an in-game reward after the advertising provider confirms that the reward was earned.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostic information. This information may be used for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalised advertising.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own policies and settings. For more information, visit the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, BrainVolt may display a consent message provided through Google's consent management tools before requesting certain advertisements. The choices available to you may depend on your location and applicable requirements. You can also review advertising and privacy settings in your device's Google settings where available.

5. Internet Connection

Core Question Game and Arrow Puzzle gameplay is designed for offline, single-player use. An internet connection may be used for advertising and related services. If advertisements are unavailable, gameplay may remain available without them.

6. Data Sharing

BrainVolt does not sell your locally stored game progress. Information processed by Google AdMob may be shared with Google and its service providers or advertising partners as described in Google's policies and subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by clearing the app's data, uninstalling the app, or using an available in-app reset option. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and settings.

8. Children's Privacy

BrainVolt is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and audience settings must be configured in accordance with the app's declared target audience and applicable child-protection laws. If you believe that information has been handled improperly, please contact us.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and its locally stored information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available at the policy URL, with the updated date shown at the top of the page.
